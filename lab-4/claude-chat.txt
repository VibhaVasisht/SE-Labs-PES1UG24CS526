⏺ I implemented on_obstacle_passed in game.py (game.py#L26-L36). Each time you
  clear an obstacle:

  - A "+1" popup appears above the obstacle, floats up and fades out over 40
    frames (about 0.7 seconds).
  - A combo counter, "Combo x N", shows under the score once you've cleared at
    least one obstacle.

  Since any hit ends the run, the combo is effectively the number of obstacles
  cleared this run. It goes back to 0 when you restart.

  How it's wired up:
  - The function has no access to the Game object, so the combo count and the
    active popups are stored at the top of the file, next to the other settings.
  - A new reset_combo() clears them, and Game.reset calls it.
  - Game.update moves and fades the popups each frame, and Game.draw draws them
    and the combo label in the same blue as the jumping dino.

  Testing: I ran the game logic in your .venv without opening a window. I held
  the dino in the air while an obstacle passed underneath. The combo went to 1
